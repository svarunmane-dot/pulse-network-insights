# Expand diagram connection points

## What will change
- Increase every device from 4 connection dots to 12: three on each side.
- Keep the existing center-dot IDs so saved diagrams and existing cables continue to work.
- Give every additional dot its own ID, allowing several independent uplinks between the same devices for VLAN, DMZ, or redundant paths.
- Keep the dots evenly spaced and large enough to connect reliably on smaller screens.

## Validation
- Open the diagram builder and connect multiple cables between the same two devices using different dots.
- Confirm existing saved and starter cables still render correctly.
